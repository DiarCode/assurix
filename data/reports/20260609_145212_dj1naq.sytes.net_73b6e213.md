## Executive Summary

A security assessment was performed on https://dj1naq.sytes.net, focusing on web application vulnerabilities and security header configurations. The assessment identified a critical lack of defense-in-depth mechanisms, most notably a potential XSS reflection and the absence of a Content-Security-Policy. These gaps increase the business risk of credential theft and unauthorized client-side execution of malicious code.

**Risk assessment**: Overall risk level: High. The combination of Potential XSS reflection and the Missing Content-Security-Policy header allows for the execution of malicious scripts in the user's browser with no mitigating controls.

**Key findings**:
- Potential XSS reflection
- Missing Content-Security-Policy header
